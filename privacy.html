Privacy notice.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice explains how the Vectorrboom portfolio website uses optional analytics. The site is operated by Abhisek / Vectorrboom. Contact: vectorrboom@gmail.com.

What is collected if you allow analytics?

If you select “Allow analytics”, Google Analytics 4 may process information such as pages visited, approximate engagement, referral/source information (for example Google, Instagram or Pinterest), campaign tags in links, browser and device category, operating system, approximate region, and interactions such as gallery filters or booking-email link clicks. Analytics is not configured to collect the contents of your emails, names, phone numbers, message text, precise GPS location or other information you type into an email.

Why is it used?

To understand overall website usage and which channels bring visitors to the portfolio, improve the site, and understand which pages or categories are useful. Reports are viewed by the site owner in the private Google Analytics account. The site does not provide visitors with access to those reports.

Consent and choice

Analytics is not loaded until you choose “Allow analytics”. If you choose “Reject”, this website will not intentionally load Google Analytics. Your choice is stored in your browser where local storage is available. You can change your choice below or clear this website’s stored data in your browser settings. Browser extensions and browser settings can also block analytics.

Retention and your rights

Analytics retention settings are managed in the Google Analytics property by the site owner. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, withdraw consent or raise a complaint. For privacy questions or requests, email vectorrboom@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.
